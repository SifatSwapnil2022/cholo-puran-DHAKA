Beauty Lassi

Lassi and lemon sherbet since 1922

FoodJohnson Road
Open on the 3D map Directions

Why go

Order the lebu sherbet: gondhoraj lemon with the slice rubbed on the rim. The lassi is made with sweet curd, and the falooda is heavy and generous. A short menu done well for a century.

History

Founded by Abdul Aziz on 15 June 1922; the original shop is at 30/1 Johnson Road, with branches in Nazira Bazar and elsewhere. Falooda was added in 2000.
