Bahadur Shah Park

A small park with a big 1857 story

HeritagePhoto spotLaxmibazar
Open on the 3D map Directions

Why go

A green pause between Sadarghat and the lanes, with a memorial to the sepoys hanged here in 1857. Refurbished in 2020 with a walking loop, benches and toilets.

History

Once the Armenian club ground 'Antaghar'. Sepoys were hanged here during the 1857 uprising, and Queen Victoria's rule was proclaimed here in 1858 (Victoria Park). Renamed after the last Mughal emperor in 1957.
