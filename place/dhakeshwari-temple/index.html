Dhakeshwari National Temple

The goddess the city may be named after

HeritageWorshipFestivalBakshibazar

Why go

Bangladesh's largest Hindu temple and Dhaka's biggest Durga Puja. Outside festival days it is peaceful, with red-ochre temple towers around a courtyard.

Festivals here

  • Durga Puja, Five days in late September or October. Dhaka's biggest Durga Puja is at Dhakeshwari, and Shankhari Bazar is lit up end to end. It ends with processions taking the idols to the river.
  • Rath Yatra, June or July, set by the Hindu calendar. Jagannath's chariot is pulled from the Swamibagh temple to Dhakeshwari.
  • Janmashtami, August or September, set by the Hindu calendar. Krishna's birthday. A procession starts at Dhakeshwari and winds through the streets of Old Dhaka. It was stopped in 1948 and has run again since 1989.

History

Traditionally dated to the 12th century and credited to Ballal Sen of the Sena dynasty, though repeated rebuilding means the present structures are later. The original idol went to Kolkata at Partition; a replica is worshipped here. Renamed the National Temple in 1996.

Photos

The temple towers across the courtyard in morning light; during Puja, the evening arati. Inside: No photos of the deity without permission.
