Chawkbazar Shahi Mosque

Shaista Khan's 1676 mosque on the market square

HeritageWorshipChawkbazar
Open on the 3D map Directions

Why go

The three-domed mosque at the heart of Chawkbazar. It anchors any walk through the market, and during Ramadan the iftar bazaar spreads out around it.

Festivals here

  • Shab-e-Barat, The night of 14–15 Shaban. Old Dhaka's night of bread and halwa. After sunset the century-old roti and halua market reopens around the Chawkbazar Shahi Mosque, and bakeries in Bangshal pile huge breads and halwa across their floors. Families share food door to door, mosques stay full, and graveyards are lit for prayers for the dead.

History

Built in 1676 by Subahdar Shaista Khan beside the Mughal market square; about 94 × 80 feet with three domes.

Photos

The domes above the market crowd from a nearby rooftop or upper floor, if a shopkeeper lets you up. Inside: Ask permission.
