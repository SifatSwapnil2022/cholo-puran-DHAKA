Lalbagh Fort

A Mughal fort that was never finished

HeritagePhoto spotLalbagh
Open on the 3D map Directions

Why go

Big lawns, red-brick gateways, and the tomb of Pari Bibi at the centre. It is the calmest large open space in Old Dhaka, which makes it a good stop between crowded lanes.

History

Begun in 1678 by Prince Muhammad Azam, son of Aurangzeb, and continued by Shaista Khan. Work stopped after his daughter Pari Bibi died in 1684. Inside are the Diwan-i-Aam with its hammam, a three-domed mosque, the tomb and a square water tank. It was called Fort Aurangabad and renamed Lalbagh Fort in 1844.
