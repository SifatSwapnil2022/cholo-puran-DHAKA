Jinjira Palace

A Mughal palace across the river, almost forgotten

HeritageHidden gemRiverKeraniganj
Open on the 3D map Directions

Why go

Across the Buriganga from Bara Katra, the remains of a late-17th-century palace hide among houses: seven rooms, two octagonal towers and a broken south gateway. Go for the story and the river crossing, not for polish.

Tip
On the Keraniganj side. Cross by nouka from the Old Dhaka bank, or by road over Babu Bazar Bridge, then ask for Jinjira Palace. Route details not yet field-checked.

History

Built in the late 17th century by Ibrahim Khan II, Bengal's last subedar before Azim-ush-Shan. Legend says a wooden bridge linked it to Bara Katra and a tunnel ran under the river to Lalbagh Fort. What survives is in very poor condition, and locals call it a haveli.
