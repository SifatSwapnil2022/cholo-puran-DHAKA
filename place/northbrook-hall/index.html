Northbrook Hall (Lalkuthi)

The red town hall on the riverbank

HeritagePhoto spotRiverFarashganj
Open on the 3D map Directions

Why go

A red Indo-Saracenic town hall from 1880, freshly restored. The outside is the reason to come; good for photos with the river behind you.

History

Funded in 1874 and inaugurated in 1880 as Dhaka's town hall. Later used as a telegraph office and a women's college. Restoration by Dhaka South City Corporation began in 2023 and was largely complete by May 2026, with a library planned in Johnson Hall.
