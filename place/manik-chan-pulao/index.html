Manik Chan's midnight pulao

Tokens at 3 am, sold out before dawn

FoodHidden gemDighubabu Lane
Open on the 3D map Directions

Why go

A pulao sold in the middle of the night from a stall with no proper shop. Tokens go out from about 3 am and when they run out, you go home hungry. For the committed only.
