Islampur cloth market

Bolts of colour behind Ahsan Manzil

MarketIslampur
Open on the 3D map Directions

Why go

A wholesale cloth district of fabric shops stacked floor to ceiling. Walk it on the way to or from Ahsan Manzil.

Hours
Shop hours, roughly 10:00 to evening.
Quiet times
Friday mornings (many shops shut)

Before Eid

Tip
Porters carry heavy loads; step aside for them.

The pin is placed by street and has not been confirmed at the entrance yet. Ask a shopkeeper for the last few metres.

Festivals here

  • Pohela Boishakh and Hal Khata, 14 April, the Bengali New Year. Old Dhaka's traders clean and decorate their shops, open a new account book (hal khata) and invite customers in for sweets, a custom that goes back to the Mughals. You will see it in the old markets around Babu Bazar.

History

One of Old Dhaka's main wholesale textile streets.
