Dhaka Collegiate School

The school where J.C. Bose and Meghnad Saha studied

HeritageHidden gemSadarghat
Open on the 3D map Directions

Why go

One of the oldest schools in the country, at the Sadarghat crossing by the Buriganga. Physicists Jagadish Chandra Bose and Meghnad Saha and the poet Buddhadeva Bose studied here. Look in from the gate on your way between Bahadur Shah Park and the river.

History

Founded on 21 June 1835 as the Dhaka English Seminary. Its old main building was demolished during redevelopment despite protests by heritage activists.
