Shakrain kite festival

Rooftops full of kites every January

FestivalPhoto spotSutrapur & Gendaria

Why go

On Poush Sankranti in mid-January, Old Dhaka's rooftops fill with families flying and fighting kites. After dark the rooftops get loud with music.

Festivals here

  • Shakrain, 14 January, the last day of Poush. Families fly and fight kites from the rooftops of Shankhari Bazar, Sutrapur, Gendaria, Narinda and Lakshmibazar, and make pitha at home. After dark it turns into fireworks, lanterns and loud music.

Crafts to see

  • Paper kites. Tanti Bazar and Shankhari Bazar in the first two weeks of January.

History

In 2026 some groups protested the festival, and police banned paper lanterns and fireworks over fire risk and limited sound systems. Check the year's rules before you go.
