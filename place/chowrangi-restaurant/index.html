Chowrangi Restaurant

Luchi and dal for breakfast, for almost a century

FoodHidden gemBanglabazar
Open on the 3D map Directions

Why go

A roadside breakfast place with a short menu: puffed luchi with house-seasoned dal topped with onion and coriander, and eggs finished with black salt. Light, cheap, and close to Beauty Boarding and the book lanes.
