Choto Katra

Shaista Khan's smaller inn, lost among shops

HeritageHidden gemChawkbazar
Open on the 3D map Directions

Why go

Bara Katra's younger sibling, 185 m to the east. Only fragments of the gateways show between shopfronts, so it is a find for people who like hunting for history.

History

Built 1663–1671 by Subahdar Shaista Khan. Restoration attempts have not succeeded, and the tomb of Champa Bibi inside is now hidden among shops.
