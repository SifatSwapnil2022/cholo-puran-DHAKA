Jagannath University

From a 1858 school to a university

Why go

A crowded, lively campus beside Bahadur Shah Park whose roots go back to 1858. It shows how Old Dhaka's education grew up around the river port.

Festivals here

  • Saraswati Puja, Vasant Panchami, in late January or February. The goddess of learning, and above all a students' festival. In Old Dhaka the puja is held in Shankhari Bazar, Tanti Bazar and at Jagannath University, with pandals in schools, temples and lanes.

History

Founded in 1858 as the Dhaka Brahma School, renamed Jagannath School in 1872 after a zamindar's father, became Jagannath College in 1884, and a university in 2005.
