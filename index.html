        About the data

        Facts come from published sources linked on every place: Wikipedia, The Daily Star, Dhaka Tribune, The Financial Express, TBS, Daily Observer, Bangladesh Monitor, Nijhoom Tours and food writers. They were collected on 12 October 2026 and have not yet been checked on site. Pins marked approximate were placed by street or neighbourhood.

        The 3D city is drawn from OpenStreetMap: about forty thousand real building footprints, every street and lane, the river banks, ponds and parks. Where OpenStreetMap has no height for a building, its storeys are estimated from its neighbours. The 62 places are modelled by hand from photographs and published descriptions, at their recorded sizes where those are known; the traffic and people are illustrative.
